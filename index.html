ii. The Model

We own nothing.
We move everything.

Morina Trading operates as an independent commodity broker — we source verified supply, qualify real demand, and structure deals that protect every party from first contact through execution.

i.

Qualify Demand

Buyer intent is confirmed before any supplier contact is made. We never approach supply without verified need on the other side.

ii.

Verify Supply

Every supplier in our network is reviewed — corporate registration, certifications, and references confirmed before engagement.

iii.

Protect Both Parties

NCND agreements are signed before any introduction is made. No names, no details exchanged without written protection in place.

iv.

Logistics is the deal.

A signed contract is the beginning, not the close. Specs, incoterms, payment structure, and inspection are all part of the same mandate.

iv. How We Work

Non-negotiable
standards.

i.

Buyer First

Demand is confirmed before any supplier is approached. We do not pitch supply into a vacuum.

ii.

Market-Verified Pricing

Every price indication is benchmarked against Platts, LME, or ICIS before it is communicated. No estimates presented as fact.

iii.

NCND Before Introduction

No counterparty identity is shared without a signed Non-Circumvention Non-Disclosure Agreement. Every deal. No exceptions.

iv.

Logistics is the trade.

A signed contract is the beginning, not the close. Freight, finance, and inspection are part of the same mandate.
